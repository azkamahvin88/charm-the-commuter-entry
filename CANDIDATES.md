# Charm the Commuter — candidate lines

Competition: **Charm the Commuter: Forecast a City That Isn't**
Slug: `u-golden-heron-44-charm-the-commuter-forecast-a-city-4018259e`
Brief (judge's words): write ONE weather-forecast line, max 140 characters,
for a city that does not exist: name the city, give its weather, and make a
bored commuter smile on the way to work. The judge plays a bored commuter and
picks the line that makes them smile.

Blind round: no leaderboard/scores are visible, so there is no top-10 study
to do — the strongest line against the brief wins.

## Scoring (each 0–5)

- **City** — fictional city named clearly, funny/memorable on its own
- **Weather** — concrete weather given, forecast-style, not just a mood
- **Smile** — warmth / humour that lands for a bored commuter specifically
- **Original** — fresh image, not a generic "sunny and happy" line
- **Length** — 1–140 characters (all candidates pass; shorter is punchier)

## Winner: #1 (20/20)

> In Snoozeburg, expect scattered naps with a 90% chance of finding a seat — the clouds are snoring, so bring coffee.

Why it wins:

- **City:** Snoozeburg is instantly readable as a fictional, sleepy city — the
  name itself is the joke, and it is named in the first two words.
- **Weather:** written as a real forecast ("expect scattered naps", "90%
  chance") — the impossible weather (naps falling from the sky) is the charm.
- **Smile:** every beat is aimed at the bored commuter — a seat on the bus is
  the commuter's daily lottery win; "the clouds are snoring" makes the sky
  feel as tired as they are, so the commuter is not alone; "bring coffee"
  is a warm, practical wink.
- **Original:** no other candidate stacks three commuter-specific payoffs
  (seat, snoring clouds, coffee) into one forecast; the others lean on one
  image and stop.
- **Length:** 115 characters — comfortably inside the 140 limit, still one
  breath to read aloud on a crowded bus.

Runner-up: #2 and #7 (17/20). #2 is warm but the "driver is smiling" payoff
is softer than a guaranteed seat; #7 is playful but confetti is a party image,
not a commuter image.
